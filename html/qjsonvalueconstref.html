Detailed Description

Its instances are what the const iterators of QJsonArray and QJsonObject return. It reads the element each time it is used, so it behaves like a const QJsonValue & and converts to QJsonValue when copied into one.

See also QJsonValueRef and QJsonValue.

Differences from Qt

There is no conversion to QVariant. A reference does not keep the container alive, and reading through one after the container changed or was destroyed is not allowed.
