Detailed Description

A margin is the distance between the edge of a rectangle and its contents. QMarginsF stores the left, top, right and bottom margins as double values. All margins are 0 by default. Use QMargins for integer margins.

See also QMargins and QRectF.

Differences from Qt

Margins are always double, which is what qreal is in Nt. There are no QDebug or QDataStream stream operators, and no operator|() or operator&() combining margins. Equality is fuzzy per margin.
