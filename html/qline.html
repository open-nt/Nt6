Detailed Description

A QLine describes a finite line (or line segment) by its two end points, stored as QPoint values. Use QLineF for floating-point coordinates and for operations such as intersection and angles.

See also QLineF and QPoint.

Differences from Qt

There are no QDebug or QDataStream stream operators.
