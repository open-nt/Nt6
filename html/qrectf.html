Detailed Description

A rectangle is stored as the position of its top-left corner and its width and height, all as double. Unlike QRect, the right and bottom edges are exclusive: right() is left() + width() and bottom() is top() + height(). Use QRect for integer coordinates.

A rectangle is valid when both its width and height are greater than 0. Use normalized() to obtain an equivalent rectangle with non-negative width and height.

See also QRect, QPointF, QSizeF and QMarginsF.

Differences from Qt

Coordinates are always double, which is what qreal is in Nt. There are no QDebug or QDataStream stream operators. The corner setters setTopLeft(), setBottomRight(), setTopRight() and setBottomLeft(), the corner movers moveTopLeft(), moveBottomRight(), moveTopRight() and moveBottomLeft(), and getRect() and getCoords() are not implemented.
