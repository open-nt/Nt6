Detailed Description

QDBusConnection::asyncCall() and QDBusAbstractInterface::asyncCall() return one. Wait for it with waitForFinished(), or watch it with a QDBusPendingCallWatcher. Copies refer to the same call.

See also QDBusPendingReply and QDBusPendingCallWatcher.

Differences from Qt

None.
