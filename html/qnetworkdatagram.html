Detailed Description

A QNetworkDatagram holds the payload of a datagram together with its sender and destination addresses and ports. QUdpSocket::receiveDatagram() returns one, and QUdpSocket::writeDatagram() sends one:

QNetworkDatagram d = socket.receiveDatagram();
socket.writeDatagram(d.makeReply("ack"));

See also QUdpSocket and QHostAddress.

Differences from Qt

The class is not implicitly shared, so copying a datagram copies its addresses (the QByteArray payload still shares its data). There is no swap() and isNull() is simply the negation of isValid().
