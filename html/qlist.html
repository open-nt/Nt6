Detailed Description

QList<T> stores its items contiguously and gives fast index-based access, fast appends and fast iteration. It is the container to reach for first.

QList<QString> names = {"Ada", "Grace"};
names << "Linus";
names.removeAll("Grace");
for (const QString &n : names)
    use(n);

See also QQueue, QStack and QVarLengthArray.

Differences from Qt

The list is a thin wrapper over std::vector, so copies are deep and it is not implicitly shared. QList<bool> is rejected with a static_assert. There is no emplace(), assign(), shrink_to_fit(), max_size(), toList() or toVector(), and no rvalue overloads of prepend() or insert(). Indexes are not range-checked except by value(). QVector is an alias of QList.
