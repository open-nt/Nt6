Detailed Description

Use it to wait for something asynchronous when QTest::qWaitFor() or QSignalSpy::wait() do not fit. Connect the event to exitLoop(), enter the loop, and check timeout():

QTestEventLoop &loop = QTestEventLoop::instance();
QObject::connect(&socket, &QTcpSocket::connected, &loop, &QTestEventLoop::exitLoop);
socket.connectToHost(QHostAddress::LocalHost, port);
loop.enterLoop(5);
QVERIFY(!loop.timeout());

Differences from Qt

enterLoopMSecs() and changeInterval() take an int number of milliseconds. There is no aboutToEnter handling of test failures.
