Detailed Description

A margin is the distance between the edge of a rectangle and its contents. QMargins stores the left, top, right and bottom margins as int values. All margins are 0 by default. Use QMarginsF for floating-point margins.

See also QMarginsF and QRect.

Differences from Qt

There are no QDebug or QDataStream stream operators, and no operator|() or operator&() combining margins. Division by an integer truncates; division by a double rounds.
