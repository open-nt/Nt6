Detailed Description

QDBusConnection::interface() returns the one of a connection. It lists and looks up the names on the bus, claims and releases names, and reports names that appear and disappear:

QDBusConnectionInterface *bus = QDBusConnection::sessionBus().interface();
if (bus->isServiceRegistered("org.example.Clock"))
    qDebug() << "owned by" << bus->serviceOwner("org.example.Clock").value();

See also QDBusConnection and QDBusServiceWatcher.

Differences from Qt

The object lives in the thread that does the connection's I/O, so its signals reach other threads through queued connections. It listens for every NameOwnerChanged signal of the bus from the moment it is created. The NameAcquired and NameLost signals are not available.
