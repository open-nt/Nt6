Turn it on in prj.conf.

Nt is a Zephyr module. Add the repository to your west manifest (or to ZEPHYR_EXTRA_MODULES), then four lines of Kconfig:

CONFIG_CPP=y
CONFIG_STD_CPP17=y
CONFIG_NT=y
CONFIG_NT_NETWORK=y

Link your application with Nt::Core and Nt::Network. The tools that run on the build machine, ntmoc and ntrcc, are compiled for the host as CMake configures a cross build, so there is nothing else to install.

https://github.com/open-nt/Nt6
