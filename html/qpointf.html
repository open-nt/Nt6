Detailed Description

A point is specified by an x coordinate and a y coordinate, both stored as double. Use QPoint for integer coordinates.

Equality comparison is fuzzy: two coordinates are considered equal if they are equal within the precision of double, as determined by qFuzzyCompare() and qFuzzyIsNull().

See also QPoint and QLineF.

Differences from Qt

Coordinates are always double, which is what qreal is in Nt. There are no QDebug or QDataStream stream operators and no platform conversions such as toCGPoint().
