# Code of Conduct

Nt follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning so that others can learn from it.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing of someone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, report it to the maintainers of the
[open-nt organization](https://github.com/open-nt) privately; do not open a public issue. Reports are kept
confidential, and the maintainers will review each one and reply to it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may
warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, a person who does so, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
