Early days. Help shape it.

An honest status for Nt on Zephyr: the sample runs and passes in QEMU, and another builds for the i.MX RT1170, but neither has run on a real board yet.

Left out, because Zephyr has one process and no desktop: QProcess, QLibrary, QSharedMemory, QSystemSemaphore, QFileSystemWatcher, QStorageInfo and local sockets. Keep to one thread where you can, as sharing objects between threads is not safe yet. Files need absolute paths, and local time is UTC.

If you have a board, try it and tell us what breaks. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
